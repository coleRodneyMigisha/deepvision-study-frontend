🏆 The Oral Exam Secret

The 3-Beat Answer Formula (Under 45 Seconds)

In a 10-minute oral exam, the professor wants to see if you actually understand the intuition or if you just memorized buzzwords. If you talk for 3 minutes without breathing, they'll interrupt you. Master the 3 Beats:

Beat 1: The Core Definition (10s)

One crisp declarative sentence defining the concept with exact keywords.

Beat 2: The Mechanism (20s)

How it actually works mechanically (parameter sharing, gradients, matrix operations).

Beat 3: Trade-off / Edge Case (15s)

When does it fail? What is the computational cost or modern alternative?
